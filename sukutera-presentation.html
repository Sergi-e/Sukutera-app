The Problem

Lake Kivu — a transboundary lake under invisible threat

Lake Kivu sits between Rwanda and DRC. Waste from Goma and Bukavu drifts continuously toward Rwandan shores — and nobody is counting it.

🐟 Sambaza Fishing

Plastic destroys sardine breeding grounds — threatening livelihoods across all 3 districts.

⚡ Ruzizi Hydro

Plastic debris clogs turbines causing measurable energy loss across the region.

📊 Zero Data

Nobody tracks what's collected, by whom, or where it goes. The system is invisible and unfundable.

🌊 Cross-Border Inflow

No permanent buffer — Rwandan shores keep receiving DRC waste indefinitely.

The Pivot

Why software — not IoT hardware

The field research revealed the core barrier is not collection — Baj Ltd already collects. The barrier is invisibility. Hardware without intelligence solves nothing.

❌ Original: IoT Sensors

Deploy sensors along the shoreline to detect plastic density.

— No data infrastructure to connect to
— Can't sort or classify waste
— High cost, low community buy-in
— Doesn't solve traceability

✅ New: Data Platform

Make the existing system measurable, incentivised, and fundable.

+ Directly addresses the data gap
+ Community-operated from day one
+ Enables future IoT on solid foundation
+ Proof of concept built and validated

The Ecosystem

Who makes this circular economy work

Sukutera sits at the centre — connecting community collectors to verified off-takers. Every company below is real, operating in Rwanda, and a potential partner in the value chain.

🏗️
Waste Collectors

Baj Ltd

Pilot Partner

Rubavu, Western Province. Local waste collector operating directly on the Lake Kivu shoreline. Verbal pilot agreement confirmed — our primary field partner.

AGRUNI Group

Founded in Rubavu

Est. 2002 by Jean Paul Ngenzi Shiraniro in Rubavu District — our exact field site. Largest waste collection and sorting company in East Africa. 1,700+ employees. Operates in Rwanda and DRC.

→ The natural aggregation partner for Sukutera's collection layer.

♻️
Plastic Recyclers

EcoPlastic Rwanda

Processes ~88 tons of plastic/year. Sources from collectors, hospitals, airports. Produces new plastic for agriculture and construction. ecoplasticrwanda.com

AgroPlast Ltd

Gahanga Industrial Zone, Kicukiro. Est. 2015. Transforms plastic waste into trash bags, agricultural tubing, and energy-saving cookstoves. agroplastrwanda.org

Depot Kalisimbi

Specifically sorts and valorizes PET and HDPE containers — the exact plastic types dominant in Lake Kivu waste. Direct match for Sukutera's sorting data. depotkalisimbi.org

🌿
Compost & Organic

GreenCare Rwanda Ltd

Converts 100% of bio-waste into "Grekompost" organic fertilizer. Promotes sorting and sale of recyclable materials. Consultancy on waste management infrastructure. greencarerwandaltd.com

Bidec Group Ltd

Kigali. Converts biodegradable market and dumpsite waste into high-quality organic compost. Sells to farmers, cooperatives, and agro-dealers at affordable prices.

Vermifarm Ltd

Founded by Vincent Sindikubwabo. Uses earthworms to produce 60 tons fertilizer + 3,600L pesticide/month from organic residues. Circular end-to-end model.
